## Users

The site serves all of these audiences (user confirmed "all"); no single one is primary:

- **Parents of children with eye conditions** — squint, suspected vision problems, prematurity (ROP), myopia, and children with special needs (cortical visual impairment, low vision). They need to trust the doctor with their child and book. *(Inferred as the largest group from the current site's paediatric focus; confirm.)*
- **Adults with squint/strabismus**, including paralytic squint and nystagmus, who are evaluating surgery or non-surgical therapy.
- **People researching a procedure** (squint surgery, paediatric cataract) who compare specialists before requesting a consultation.
- **Families returning for ongoing care** — vision therapy, myopia control and follow-ups across generations (a published testimonial describes the whole family, including grandparents, consulting the doctor).

Referring doctors are not a confirmed audience.

## Product Purpose

The public website of **Dr Aloka's Eye Care**, the clinic of **Dr. Aloka Hedau** in Kukatpally, Hyderabad. It exists to help people understand the clinic's specialist paediatric and squint care, trust the doctor, and book an appointment. Success means visitors book an appointment (online, by phone or by email) with a clear understanding of which service fits their need.

## Product Principles

1. **Specialist first.** Every page reinforces that this is a dedicated paediatric and squint practice, not a general eye clinic.
2. **Reassure the parent, respect the adult.** Content speaks to anxious caregivers without talking down to adult squint patients.
3. **Always one step from booking.** Online, phone and email options stay easy to find from any page.
4. **Real evidence only.** Credentials, statistics, photos and reviews come from the clinic; nothing is invented or embellished.
5. **Explain before recommending surgery.** Show both non-surgical and surgical paths plainly so families can make informed decisions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The practice treats low vision, cortical visual impairment and nystagmus, so some visitors (or the caregivers helping them) will have impaired vision. *(Inferred requirement; confirm the target standard.)* Treat WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor, with particular care for text size and zoom, contrast, screen-reader support and not relying on colour alone.
