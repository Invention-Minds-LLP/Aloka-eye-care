So Pediatric Ophthalmologists also work hand in hand with Geneticists, Pediatric Neurologists, Pediatric Endocrinologists, Pediatric Psychiatrist, Gastroenterologists and Physiotherapists.

It is Preferable to see a Pediatric Ophthalmologist for your child as

- There are specialized charts for Non-Verbal children or special children to assess the vision

- The Refraction evaluation is more accurate due to vast experience in non-subjective way of assessing children

- Stereoacuity and Binocularity are evaluated

- The Convergence and Accommodation (which are functions of the visual system) are also specially evaluated

- Preventive Medical Care in form of exercises, vision therapy and diet changes are also available

- The doctors in this speciality are trained with and molded to be very patient and empathic towards patient care that involves the child, parent and care givers.

- Surgeries on kids’ eyes requires much more precision and planning for the whole life of the child as is learnt by these professionals.
