Preparing for a visit

1

Bring previous reports

Carry any relevant eye reports, prescriptions or investigation results.

2

Understand the assessment

The evaluation may include vision tests, eye alignment checks and other required assessments.

3

Discuss the next steps

Your treatment or management plan will be explained based on the assessment.

What should I bring to the appointment?
  • Any glasses your child (or you) currently wears
  • Previous eye reports, prescriptions and scan or test results
  • A list of current medicines, and any relevant medical history
  • For little ones, a favourite toy or snack helps keep them comfortable
