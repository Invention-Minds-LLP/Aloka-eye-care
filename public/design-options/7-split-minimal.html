01 — Care

What we treat

Squint in children

Therapy and surgery, ideally before age 8, while vision is still developing.

Lazy eye

Bynocs binocular vision therapy; 10–15 sessions are usually enough.

Myopia control

Axial-length tracking, lifestyle advice and medication where it helps.

Paediatric cataract

Phacoemulsification with lens implant, done promptly.

ROP screening

For babies born before 34 weeks or under 1750 g.

Adult squint & nystagmus

Micro-incision and adjustable-suture surgery. Age is no bar.
