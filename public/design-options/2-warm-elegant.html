Treatments

What we treat

Therapy first, wherever it can work. Surgery only when it is the right answer.

  1. Squint in children

    Therapy and surgery, ideally before age 8, while binocular vision is developing.

    Children

  2. Lazy eye & vision therapy

    Bynocs binocular software and fusion work, 30–45 minute sessions.

    Children

  3. Myopia control

    Axial-length tracking, lifestyle advice and medication where it helps.

    Children

  4. Paediatric cataract

    Phacoemulsification with lens implant, done promptly.

    Children

  5. Prematurity screening (ROP)

    For babies born before 34 weeks or under 1750 g.

    Babies

  6. Adult squint & double vision

    Sutureless and adjustable-suture techniques. Age is no bar.

    Adults

  7. Nystagmus surgery

    Eases the head posture, strain and neck discomfort.

    All ages

Equipment

Precise instruments, gentle tests.

Myopia tracking

Pristine OBM 12

Measures the length of the eye, the curve of the cornea and the glasses power, so any increase in myopia is caught early.

Cornea mapping

Sirius+ topography

Maps the shape of the cornea point by point, for a detailed picture of the eye's front surface.
