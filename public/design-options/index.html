Dr. Aloka's Eye Care: design options

Seven new home page designs: four light and three dark. Each one uses the clinic's real content and photos: patient results, the equipment and appointment booking. They differ in layout, colours and fonts. Please pick one, or mix parts of each.

New · Recommended

Signboard

Built only on the clinic's logo colours. The site works like a premium bilingual hospital signboard: solid teal sign panels, an orange "Book appointment" sign, direction plates for each condition, and English with Telugu. Parents start from what they notice in their child ("One eye turns in…") and the sign leads them to the right treatment. The font is Atkinson Hyperlegible, designed for people with low vision.
