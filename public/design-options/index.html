Dr. Aloka's Eye Care: design options

Seven new home page designs: four light and three dark. Each one uses the clinic's real content and photos: patient results, the equipment and appointment booking. They differ in layout, colours and fonts. Please pick one, or mix parts of each.

Newest · Cinematic 3D

Two Pictures Become One

A cinematic dark page built around the synoptophore, the instrument used in squint care. Scrolling flies the camera into its eyepiece, then each condition is shown as two glass slides, one per eye, that fuse into one picture: the bird lands in the cage (squint), the fish swims into the bowl (lazy eye), and the Charminar comes into focus under the kite (myopia). Then Dr. Aloka is revealed through the lens, followed by real results, the clinic and booking. Layered background parallax throughout, no live 3D, so it scrolls smoothly.

Newest · Full 3D + glass

Glass

The complete home page as one live 3D scene behind frosted-glass panels. The glossy eyes and glass spheres move from scene to scene as you scroll: a cluster where one squinting eye straightens; a big pair of eyes that follows your mouse, with buttons to show an inward or outward squint; a blurred eye chart with a glass magnifier that brings the letters into focus; and a finale where every eye circles the page and looks at "Book an appointment". Best viewed on a computer first, then on a phone.

Open Glass →
Live 3D

Living Eyes

In the style of award-winning 3D studio websites: the page opens on a live 3D window full of glossy eyes in the logo's teal and orange. Every eye follows your mouse, except one that keeps looking away, like a squint. As you scroll, that eye is "treated" and turns to look with the others. The rest of the page is the premium white design. Best viewed on a computer first, then on a phone.

Open Living Eyes →
Premium + interactive

Vision

The premium white hospital look, with four interactive moments only an eye clinic could have: a squinting pair of eyes that straightens as you scroll, eyes that follow your mouse with a "show a squint" button, a blurry classroom board with a lens that shows how a child with myopia sees, and drag-to-compare before/after photos. Best viewed on a computer first, then on a phone.

Open Vision →
Latest · Premium hospital

Premium Hospital

In the style of Apollo, Rainbow and Moorfields: mostly white and open, led by large photos. Dr. Aloka's portrait opens the page, followed by a quick-action bar (Book, Call, WhatsApp, Hours), photo tiles for each speciality, a before/after slider, a photo tour of the clinic and reviews. Logo teal and orange are used only as accents.

New · Recommended

Signboard

Built only on the clinic's logo colours. The site works like a premium bilingual hospital signboard: solid teal sign panels, an orange "Book appointment" sign, direction plates for each condition, and English with Telugu. Parents start from what they notice in their child ("One eye turns in…") and the sign leads them to the right treatment. The font is Atkinson Hyperlegible, designed for people with low vision.
