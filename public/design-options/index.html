Dr. Aloka's Eye Care: design options

Two directions for the home page.

Two Pictures Become One

Cinematic 3D motion page built around the synoptophore. The camera flies into the eyepiece, then each condition is two glass slides, one per eye, that fuse into one picture: the bird lands in the cage (squint), the fish swims into the bowl (lazy eye), the Charminar comes into focus under the kite (myopia). Then Dr. Aloka, an auto-playing "first visit" guide, the clinic and booking. No patient photos.

The illustrated story

The family journey told with illustrated characters: the little girl, Amma, Ajji and Dr. Aloka, from noticing the squint to the treatment, with parallax scenes that also play by themselves.
