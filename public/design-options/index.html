Cinematic 3D page. Two eyepieces roll together into one; that lens rolls down into the clinic's Sirius+ topographer, then frames Dr. Aloka's photo. Then a 3D services carousel, the auto-playing first-visit steps, Google reviews and the visit details. No patient photos.
