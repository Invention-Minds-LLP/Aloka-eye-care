Specialities

Care, precisely for children

I.

Squint

Therapy and surgery for children, ideally before age 8, and adult correction at any age.

II.

Lazy eye

Bynocs binocular vision therapy, 30–45 minute sessions; 10–15 are usually enough.

III.

Myopia control

Axial-length tracking, lifestyle advice and medication where it helps.

IV.

Paediatric cataract

Phacoemulsification with lens implant, done promptly to protect vision.

V.

Prematurity (ROP)

Screening for babies born before 34 weeks or under 1750 g.

VI.

Nystagmus

Surgery that eases head posture, strain and neck discomfort.
