Have you noticed any of these?

Children rarely say they can't see well. These signs are worth an eye check.

One eye wanders

In photos, or when tired, one eye turns in, out, up or down.

Squeezing & watering

Frequent squeezing or watering can point to a power problem.

A tilted head

Tilting or turning the head to read or watch TV.

Double vision (adults)

Seeing two of everything, headaches and strain while reading.
