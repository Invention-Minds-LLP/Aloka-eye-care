Your visit

Simple, from first call to clear sight

Open Monday to Saturday, 10 am – 5 pm. Children are checked from six months of age.

  1. Book

    Book online, call or WhatsApp. Pick a time that suits your family.

    Picture charts and play-based tests, so even toddlers can be checked.

    Dr. Aloka explains what she found and what will help, in plain words.

  4. Treatment

    Glasses, vision therapy or surgery. Therapy first, surgery only when it is right.
