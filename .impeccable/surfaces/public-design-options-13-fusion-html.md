# Surface brief: 13-fusion.html (home page concept)

Scope: home page design concept, static HTML in public/design-options/. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: parents of children with squint, lazy eye or myopia (often on phones, anxious); adults with squint. Job: understand that this is the specialist in eyes working together, trust Dr. Aloka, book.
Proof: real doctor photo, credentials, real before/after outcomes, real clinic photos. Generated imagery is limited to the instrument, glass slides and background plates, labelled illustrative.
Constraints (user, 2026-11-12): cinematic and immersive (dark allowed); one rendered hero object with zooms and reveals; background parallax; no live WebGL; must scroll smoothly; nothing toy-like; nothing unsettling for parents.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Squint care is teaching two eyes to make one picture. The page is a synoptophore: every chapter is two glass slides, one per eye, that fuse as you scroll (bird into cage, fish into bowl, kite over Charminar). It refuses the eye-hospital default of a macro eyeball photo on white.

OWN-WORLD: A dimmed orthoptic room. Blue-black night ground (#08131a), enamel white instrument, polished steel slide rims, warm backlit glass, teal (#1f7fa3) as the only cool light, logo orange (#e06a24) reserved for booking. Round lit slides are the only frames; type sits like instrument engraving, quiet and precise.

STORY: See the instrument, understand fusion through three treatable conditions, meet Dr. Aloka through the eyepiece, see real results fuse before/after, see the clinic, book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed dark stage with three parallax plates. The instrument stands large right of centre, lit; headline left at display scale; Book (orange) and Call beside it; a thin chapter progress line along the bottom. Scrolling pushes the camera into the right eyepiece, which becomes the first slide.

FORM: Impeccable's pick, candidate 1 of 7 ("Two Pictures Become One", synoptophore fusion slides); seed key 591b8a64. Raises: one lit progress line always shows the chapter; oversized chapter numerals with few words; the slide pair stays whole and centred at every size.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
