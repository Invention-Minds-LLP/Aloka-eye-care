# Home page

Scope: the public home route of the Angular rebuild. Mode: Persuade.

Audience/job: Hyderabad parents (often deciding with grandparents) and adult squint patients who arrive worried. They must see their own situation, trust Dr. Aloka Hedau, and book, call or WhatsApp. Proof: real credentials and affiliations, clinic-stated figures, named testimonials, the before/after published on the site, FAQ answers (pain, scars, age, the "squint is lucky" myth).

User-pinned brief (2026-09-26, after rejecting the kite-sky and trial-lens worlds): a literal, professional story told with illustrated characters. A worried parent and child arrive, meet the doctor, find hope in the consultation, get treated, and leave happy. Rich background parallax and animation, a light theme, appeal to both parents and kids, the doctor's specialities, a design grounded in how Hyderabad families think, a few Telugu lines, and a WhatsApp action.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One family's visit, told in four illustrated scenes you scroll through: worried arrival, consultation, treatment, a happy exit. It refuses both the stock-photo clinic homepage and abstract metaphor.

OWN-WORLD: A flat, warm vector illustration of Hyderabad in daylight. KPHB streets, the elevated metro, an auto-rickshaw, apartment blocks, a small Charminar on the horizon. Indian characters: mother in a saree, a schoolgirl, and Dr. Aloka in a white coat over a saree. The scene palette warms from muted grey-blue to sunny. Logo teal marks structure, logo orange is for hope and the Book key, WhatsApp green is for chat only. Bricolage Grotesque display, Hanken Grotesk body, Anek Telugu for Telugu lines.

STORY: Arrive (worry, myths) → Consult (Dr. Aloka listens and examines) → Treat (therapy first, surgery if right, sticker chart fills, eyes align) → Leave happy (families' words) → specialities → why Hyderabad families trust us → visit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Scene 1, full-bleed. A muted street before the clinic with parallax layers. Mother and daughter at lower centre-right, heads low. H1 and the Telugu line upper-left. Book (orange), WhatsApp and Call beneath.

FORM: User-pinned literal story. No roll; the brief beats the roll. Previous seed 4f28dd1b superseded.
