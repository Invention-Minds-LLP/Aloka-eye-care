Scope: home page concept, Persuade mode. Parents (often on phones) and adult squint patients must understand this is a paediatric + squint specialist and book.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The clinic as a bilingual wayfinding system. Every section is a sign that tells a worried parent where to go next. Refuses the template hospital page (stock hero, stat row, icon-card grid, eyebrow labels).

OWN-WORLD: Painted enamel sign panels in logo teal #2a8cb0 (deep #1d6c8a) and logo orange #e06a24 (action #c4551a) on white; solid fields own whole regions; 6px radius plates with rivet dots; arrows as the only ornament; English with Telugu beneath on every major sign. One face: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (low-vision legibility), Anek Telugu for Telugu.

STORY: The visitor sees which sign matches their child, sees proof (results, doctor, machines), and follows the orange sign to book, call or WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width teal sign panel: large white headline left, Telugu line under it, doctor photo mounted as a plate right. Below, a direction board row: white condition plates with arrows on the left, the orange "Book appointment" plate on the right with hours.

FORM: Bilingual signage/wayfinding, candidate 6 of 7; seed f3b5879d. Raises: from the seed-packet rack, every condition plate leads with the symptom parents notice, then the treatment path; from teletext, one strict modular plate grid, every plate sized from the same unit.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
